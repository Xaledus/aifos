AIFOS

Consulting expertise. A delivery mindset.

AIFOS supports organisations in transforming their business functions, processes, data and systems.

Our approach rests on a simple conviction:

The value of consulting is measured by its ability to produce a result.

We combine business understanding, project management, data expertise and command of technology environments to support transformations from initial scoping through to implementation.

Consulting

Understand the challenges, challenge the requirements and bring the expertise needed to decide.

Transformation

Evolve processes, organisations, data and solutions in a consistent way.

Delivery

Turn strategy and decisions into operational results.

Our model

A senior, hands-on approach.

AIFOS favours a senior, agile approach, directly involved in the projects.

Our growth is built on proven expertise and on the progressive development of a network of skills, allowing us to adapt our capacity to the needs of each engagement.

We prioritise the quality of the expertise and the relevance of the people involved over the number of resources deployed.

Our principles

Four operating principles.

  1. 01

    Understand before proposing

    Every organisation has its own constraints, processes and challenges.

  2. 02

    Simplify complexity

    Make problems understandable so that decisions and action become possible.

  3. 03

    Connect the disciplines

    Business, Management, Data and Technology should not work in silos.

  4. 04

    Stay outcome-driven

    Transformation must produce a concrete, measurable impact.
